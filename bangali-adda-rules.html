More than a WhatsApp group.

Bangali Adda started with a simple idea: bringing Bengali families in Lucknow closer together. From cultural events and Bong Fest to shared support and friendship, our community is built on connection, not competition.

Everyone deserves an adda where they belong. These guidelines help us keep it that way.

📌 The group code

The adda playbook.

11 simple guidelines to keep the group useful, inclusive and full of good energy.

RULE 01🎉

All about the adda

Healthy addabazi, cultural discussions, community updates, event information and bonding among Bengalis of the city — that's why we're here.

Our purpose
RULE 02🫶

Respect is non-negotiable

Be kind to every member. No personal attacks, offensive language or disrespectful comments.

Good vibes
RULE 03🕊️

Skip the heated debates

No political arguments, controversial religious debates or sensitive-topic fights. Let's keep the peace.

Harmony first
RULE 04🚫

No forward-fest, please

Avoid unnecessary forwards, fake news, chain messages and unrelated viral content. Quality over quantity!

Keep it relevant
RULE 05📸

Media, but make it mindful

Shared images and videos will be deleted after a few hours to reduce clutter. Put personal photos, reels and videos on Facebook or Instagram instead.

Media policy
RULE 06💬

Less oversharing, more adda

Keep personal updates occasional so the group stays informative, organized and easy to follow.

Thoughtful posting
RULE 07📣

Promos need a green light

Business promotions, services and advertisements are allowed only through Srijon or with prior admin permission.

Admin approval
RULE 08🎭

Events? Absolutely yes!

Share Bangali Adda events, cultural programs, meetings and other relevant updates for Bengalis in the city.

Community updates
RULE 09🌈

Any language. Kind tone.

Bangla, Hindi or English — all are welcome. Stay polite, friendly and inclusive with every message.

Inclusive space
RULE 10🤝

Take conflicts off-chat

Resolve issues privately or contact the admins. Please avoid public arguments in the group.

Peaceful resolution
RULE 11🛡️

Admins have the final say

Admins may delete posts or remove members who disturb the group's decorum. Let's respect their decisions.

Community moderation
